honesty (K1–K7):
K1 the breaking point is modeled as a phase change / bistable barrier (dynamical-systems metaphor) — the inset well is illustrative, not a fitted potential.
K2 the institutional effect is the well-evidenced flip side of Gambetta/Varese/Tilly: rule of law + accessible credit RAISE the defection threshold and stop contagion. Build trust → the protection market shrinks.
K3 two knobs do DIFFERENT jobs: credit/safety-net stops death (rescues viable owners); rule of law stops predation (catches predators, protects neighbors). You need both.
K4 credit only rescues the viable — a structurally-unprofitable flower a loan can't save (honest, and true).
K5 "breaking" = desperation-driven defection under weak institutions. This is NOT "poor people are criminals" — the whole point is that institutions, not character, set the line.
K6 governance quality is measurable (World Bank WGI) and BUILDABLE — not any country's destiny.
K7 illustrative agent model, not a calibrated prediction. Tune to reality.
