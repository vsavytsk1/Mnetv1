K3 Pillar Two (2024+): a 15% global minimum effective tax. Route profit to a sub-15% haven and a red TOP-UP flow claws the difference back. The old haven play hits a hard floor. Current law, not a trick.

K5 ATAD interest cap: interest is deductible only up to ~30% of EBITDA. Over-lever the group and the excess debt buys no shield.
