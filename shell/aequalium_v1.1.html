The equals sign transcends this reality

An experiment in the quantum realm or the galactic realm hands you data — a curve f(t). To say model = data, you must reproduce that curve with something you can actually compute. Here the compute is a pure Fourier sum, and the number of harmonics you may spend is set by geometry: the face count of a live Goldberg buckyball grown from C60.

The curve trapped in the C60

Grow the bucky (add hexagons, the 7 real ops from GENESIS) and you buy more harmonics K. K = floor(faces / 2), capped by the sample Nyquist. Each growth drops the residual: more compute, more spini-spini geometry, more degrees of certainty. P=12 pentagons are the twelve lowest fundamental modes; the hexagons are the overtones you grow.

Why it is never exactly "="

At a jump, Fourier overshoots forever (Gibbs). The residual falls toward zero but never reaches it in finite K. So the badge reads the measured 1 - L2 residual, never a hard-coded 100%. Target is not result.

Discrete transform (computed live, every change)

Sample f at tm = 2πm/M, m=0..M-1.
ck = (1/M) ∑m f(tm) e-i k tm,   k = -K..K.
recon(t) = ∑k=-KK ck ei k t.
residual = ||f - recon||2 / ||f||2.

Epicycles = spini-spini

Each ck is a rotating vector of radius |ck| and speed k. Chain them tip-to-tail and the tip traces recon(t). That chain of turning circles is the Fourier series drawn as geometry — the CURVE view shows it live for the closed C60-silhouette target (the literal curve trapped in the bucky).

Kernel invariants (from topology, not float-matching)

V = (5P + 6H)/3,   E = (5P + 6H)/2,   chi = V - E + F. A clean refineAll keeps P = 12 exactly and chi = 2 exactly, at every size. Shown live, never asserted.

What is real

The buckyball, its face/vertex/edge counts and chi come from the REAL goldberg_kernel.js, injected verbatim (view source and grep). The DFT, the residual and the certainty are all computed in this tab, from the samples, and displayed with the number that is actually measured.

What is illustrative

The target curves (square / sawtooth / triangle / pulse / C60 silhouette) are stand-ins for experimental data, chosen because their exact Fourier behaviour is known, so you can watch the residual fall honestly. This sim predicts no physical measurement; it demonstrates the method by which compute is matched to data.
