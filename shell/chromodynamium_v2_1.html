For anyone who checks things for a living

Computed at runtime, from the matrices

The eight Gell–Mann matrices are the only physics input in the file. View source and grep: there is no 4/3, no C_A=3, no root coordinates. All of it is derived:

TF ← Tr(TaTb) = TFδab
CF ← ∑aTaTa = CF𝕀
fabc ← [Ta,Tb] = i fabcTc
CA ← facdfbcd = CAδab
weights ← diagonal of T3, T8
roots ← differences of fundamental weights

Residuals are displayed beside every value, not folded away.

The hexagon

The six non-zero roots come out at exactly 0°, 60°, 120°, 180°, 240°, 300°, all of length 1.000000. A regular hexagon, plus two zero-weights at the origin (the Cartan subalgebra) — eight gluons.

Honest note: this hexagon is the root lattice of su(3). It is not the hexagon of a Goldberg polyhedron, and nothing here claims they are the same object. What they share is the kind of necessity: both shapes are forced by a symmetry rather than chosen. Euler forces twelve pentagons; the Weyl group forces sixty degrees. Resemblance is not identity, and this sim does not trade on it.

Input, not derived

αs(MZ) = 0.1180 is a measured world average, and MZ = 91.1876 GeV likewise. ΛQCD is obtained by inverting the 1-loop solution — it is a consequence of that measurement, not an independent result. Nothing here predicts αs.

Where the curve is lying

Below ~1 GeV perturbation theory does not apply. The curve is drawn into that region so the divergence is visible, and the region is shaded, because the pole is the approximation failing, not a physical infinity. Confinement is real; the Landau pole is an artefact of truncating at one loop.

Scheme and order

1-loop and 2-loop are both drawn; the gap between them is the honest size of the truncation. No quark-threshold matching is performed — nf is held fixed by the slider rather than stepped at mc, mb, mt. That is a simplification, and it is stated rather than hidden.

The join

M₃(C) is one object seen three ways: PLATEAUM (a node on the subalgebra chain, Dirac dim 128, dropping to the 16 plateau when C or H is added) · CHROMODYNAMIUM (its traceless anti-hermitian part is su(3): 8 gluons, CA=3) · LAGRANGIUM (sector 1, where CA≠0 produces the fabc vertices).

What this is not

A teaching and verification instrument. It derives standard results from standard definitions. It contains no new physics and claims none.
