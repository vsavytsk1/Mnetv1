WHAT THE FOLD IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT
EXACT: the C60 graph is Hamiltonian, so 60 tokens map onto it with every consecutive pair landing on a real edge. Audited at boot — 60 visited, 0 repeats, 0 non-edges. Locality is perfect, not approximate.
DESIGN CHOICE: this is one Hamiltonian cycle of many. A different one draws a different picture and is equally valid.
DECLARED ARTIFACT: a cycle wraps — token 59 sits beside token 0. Text does not wrap. That closing edge is drawn dashed red: a lie the geometry tells, not one the data told.
LIMIT: 60 slots. Longer prompts are truncated, and the HUD says by how many.
MEASURED WEAKNESS: chord LENGTH carries almost no meaning. Correlation between sequence gap and geometric distance is r = +0.115 over all 1,770 pairs — a gap of 10 and a gap of 45 land at similar distances. The fold guarantees adjacency and essentially nothing beyond it. Read the chords as connections, never as distances.
