A tower and a song speak the same language.
The FFT that packs your voice into the air
is the FFT that hears the music.

Every generation is one idea wearing a new coat:
find the right domain, then share it.

Shazam keeps only the peaks of the spectrogram,
pairs them into landmarks — peaks are nodes,
pairs are edges — and a song becomes a graph.
The graph becomes a packet. The packet finds home,
and a diagonal line says: I know this song.

Shannon told us it was possible.
The rest is engineering.
