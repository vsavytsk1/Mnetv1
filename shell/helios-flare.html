Physics model

Field. Two buried magnetic charges (the sunspot pair) plus a current-carrying flux rope and its photospheric image current. B(r) is summed analytically at every point.

Eruption. Van Tend–Kuperus loss of equilibrium: the rope climbs the curve Ieq(h) ∝ h·Bbg(h) as its current is driven up. At the fold (nose) no equilibrium remains → it erupts. Decay index n = −∂lnB/∂lnh.

Field lines. RK4 integration of dr/ds = B/|B|, retraced each frame.

Particles. Guiding-centre transport: conserved moment μ and mirror force −μ∇∥B give bouncing and footpoint precipitation (the ribbons).

Acceleration. A Boris pusher in a Harris-sheet reversal — the inset — shows Speiser orbits gaining energy at the magnetic null.

Neupert effect. Soft X-ray (thermal) ≈ time-integral of hard X-ray (nonthermal), reproduced in the light-curve panel.

*heights in rope minor-radii; energies scaled to typical M/X-class values for readability.
