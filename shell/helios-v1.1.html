Bow to the titans of the sun

Richard Carrington — 1859. Saw the first solar flare in white light; 17 h later the sky burned to the tropics. The event bears his name.
Kristian Birkeland — the terrella. Argued currents flow down the field into the poles. Mocked for decades; the currents are now his.
Chapman & Ferraro — 1931. A cloud of solar plasma carves a cavity in Earth's field. The magnetopause, and its current, are theirs.
Hannes Alfvén — magnetohydrodynamics; the wave that carries tension along a field line. Nobel 1970. The plasma and the field move as one.
Eugene Parker — 1958. Predicted a supersonic solar wind and its spiral when everyone said the space between worlds was empty. The Probe that touched the Sun is his.
James Dungey — 1961. Southward IMF reconnects on the dayside, opens the field, and closes it again down the tail. The master key of this whole sim.
Syun-Ichi Akasofu — read the auroral substorm as one machine: growth, onset, expansion, recovery. The lights are a readout.

The honest debt

a flare is a magnetic reconnection nobody can yet predict from first principles — it takes an absurd amount of compute to even try, and the sun still surprises us. these minds spent lifetimes on the bonkers magneto-fusion dynamics of one ordinary star. this render bows to them.

The chain, link by link

1 the sun erupts — a CME: a magnetized plasma cloud, launched with its own frozen-in Bz.
2 it arrives — density & speed jump. Pdyn=1.6726e-6·n·v² rises; the magnetopause is crushed sunward.
3 the clock angle decides. θc=atan2(By,Bz) from north. South = 180°.
4 coupling ∝ v·Bt·sin⁴(θc/2). Northward → the door is shut. Southward → it opens.
5 dayside flux reconnects, peels over the poles, loads the tail (Dungey).
6 the tail snaps — substorm. Dipolarization. The oval floods equatorward.
7 the ring current grows: dDst*/dt = Q − Dst*/τ (Burton 1975). Dst plunges.
8 the ground field lurches. dB/dt drives currents in anything long and conducting.

What is real vs schematic

the dipole, r=L·sin²θ, is exact and closed (∇·B=0). the standoff (Chapman–Ferraro) and the boundary (Shue 1997) are the cited models, computed live. the compression & tail are a schematic of the true distorted field — flagged, not faked.

Carrington, 1859 — telegraph lines arced; operators worked on aurora-current alone. A repeat on today's grid is a trillion-dollar question.
The mechanism — a long conductor (a power line, a pipeline, a telegraph) sees a changing B. Faraday: an EMF appears. GIC ∝ dB/dt · ground response. Transformers run hot, saturate, and can fail.
this is why dB/dt, not the pretty aurora, is the number the grid operator watches.

Why this stays hard

a flare's trigger is a reconnection in a field we can't measure inside the sun. the arrival time of a CME has a real uncertainty of ±6–12 hours even with today's models; whether it does damage rides on a single number — the sign of Bz — which we usually only learn ~30–60 minutes ahead, when the cloud reaches L1. running the coupled sun→heliosphere→magnetosphere→ground chain as an ensemble costs a stupid amount of compute, and the sun still surprises us. so this panel shows the spread and refuses to print a single confident hour. that refusal is the honest part.
bow to the difficulty. that is the whole ethic: pay the price in compute to see, and never fake the receipt.
