off-grid cooling — the measured numbers

real practitioner + lab figures. each tagged by where it actually works. all metric.
salt-brine pull-down works anywhere
warm 0.33 L can → drinkable in ~2 min (vs ~15 in plain ice-water). SINTEF 2025. the fast lane — but burns ice quicker. keep a small salted bucket separate from the hold cooler.
block vs cubed ice works anywhere
same mass: block holds ~3.5 d vs cubes ~3 d. block core for longevity + cubes in the gaps for fast chill = the pro move.
dead chest freezer = the box works anywhere
insulation R ≈ 14–20, beats most coolers, free. stand it up, working drain. do NOT run a frost-free unit wrapped in insulation — overheats. dead-box-with-ice only.
ice budget works anywhere
plan ~0.7 kg/guest baseline, ~1 kg on a hot day. ~0.1 kg just to pull one warm can down. pre-chill stock the night before. 2:1 ice:contents, fill the air gaps.
jockey box (draft) works anywhere
keg beer 21 → 3 °C at the tap, ice-bath coil + CO₂. coil type for volume; keep the keg itself on ice to avoid foam.
propane absorption fridge works anywhere
genuine ~4 °C, ~0.45 kg gas/day, no moving parts. must be level; slow pull-down (12–24 h); loses capacity in extreme heat. the one true off-grid "fridge".
evaporative (zeer / wet burlap) dry only
8–15 °C below ambient in DRY air; <4 °C if humid. floor ≈ wet-bulb. cools PRODUCE, never beer to cold. useless in a humid coastal town — see "this event".
night-sky radiative novelty
~5 °C below ambient, clear dry nights. ancient yakhchāl ice-making. real physics, poor day-of-event tool.

honesty — what's real, what's flagged

flags:
works anywhere physics holds in any climate.
dry only dies as humidity rises — the wet-bulb is a hard floor.
novelty real but not a practical vendor tool.

the wet-bulb floor: evaporative cooling can never beat the wet-bulb temperature. Stull (2011) approximation, computed live from your ambient + RH. In humid air the wet-bulb sits near the air temp, so evaporative does almost nothing.

latent heat: ice absorbs L_f = 334 kJ/kg melting — the whole cooling budget, countable. Salt brine reaches the eutectic −21.1 °C @ 23.3 wt% (and the salt slider is clamped there).

sources: SINTEF salt-chill test 2025 · America's Test Kitchen brine ≈ −8 °C · Engel/YETI ice-retention · Homebrewtalk chest-freezer R-value · FAO/MIT D-Lab zeer limits · Stull, J.Appl.Meteor.Climatol. 2011.

numbers are typical ranges, not promises. measure your own box with a thermometer before betting stock on it.
