ARACNIUM v2.0 — HEAVE-NET
Every spider is a node; threads in reach are edges — the telaraña. ASK the swarm (or click the cube) and the graph does the rest: it resolves the spiders nearest the cube by their composite IP (geohash·clock), finds the shortest path with landmarks — the same trick Shazam uses — recruits them, and they heave the cube to ✦. Detail isn't free: more detail = more compute = slower. Push past the budget and the screen floods until you pull it back.

HOW DOES THE SWARM KNOW WHAT TO DO?

address = geohash(x,y) · clock(t)
route = argmin Σ wᵢ (shortest path)
landmarks: |d(L,a) − d(L,b)| ≤ d(a,b)
No spider is in charge. There is only the graph.
Each spider knows its place and time — that's its address —
and the threads to its neighbors. Nothing more.

You ask. The request finds the nearest spiders by their IP,
then walks the shortest path to them — and to go fast
it leans on a few landmarks, exactly as Shazam leans on
landmark pairs to find a song. Same mathematics, different stage.

Then the recruited spiders lean in together and the cube moves.
All of it is nodes and edges. The detail you see is paid for
in compute — ask for too much and the whole thing seizes.

Math is the base. From there, you pay to see.
