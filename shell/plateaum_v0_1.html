What is NOT shown here

The centre of this picture is an open question, not a result:

Does a variational functional on finite real spectral triples at KO-dimension 6 have A_F as its unique critical point, while encoding the gauge content?

Nobody has closed this. The plateau locates it precisely — three algebras tie at 16, so dimension alone cannot select A_F. Something else must, or nothing does.

Path II — render the rim, honour the centre, never display it. The gap on the plot is drawn open on purpose.

What this is not

This is not a derivation of the Standard Model. The maximalist "Reality Generator" hypothesis failed against the Choi–Effros–Størmer theorem and parameter counting. That failure is preserved in the repository beside what survived. This sim draws only what survived.
