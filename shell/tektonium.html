A young person is not fighting talent — they are fighting a landscape. Born low, in the trap valley: the accumulated bias, the pull to leave, the world with no ladder. Left alone, the bead just rolls to the bottom of the trap and stays. Almost none cross.

You do not drag each kid over the ridge. You bend the surface: pipeline lowers the ridge and deepens the mastery valley (without a real job, mastery is a mirage — skilled, then emigrates). A certified master adds the uphill pull. Wrap-around steadies the roll so fewer fall back. Legibility — the reverse-meme — thins the home-bias headwind on the kid and the parents. Then the roll runs the other way, and a majority cross — but never all. The tail that stays down is the honesty (Kluve: effects are real but modest).

And you must keep the mastery valley deep: persist = rev + (1−rev)·e^(−yrs/τ). Starve the earned revenue and the valley fills in over the years — the beads that crossed roll back out. That is the donor cliff, drawn honest (Blattman 2020: the gains dissipated). Squeeze apprentice labour for revenue instead of teaching, and it's a sweatshop — the pull dies.

The deepest fact: the chain of unbroken social pain from the very first report was a loop with the wrong sign. The program works when it closes a reinforcing loop of the right sign — b1 flips 0→1 the instant skill couples to real work. Same graph-theoretic object. Flip it.

Honour the titans of building by handing the next set of hands the same landscape they climbed — only with the ridge cut lower. L = E − V + C · the loop is the mechanism · always.
