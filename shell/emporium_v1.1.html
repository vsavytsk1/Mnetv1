honesty (K1–K7) — real institutional physics, honest limits:
K1 a TEACHING model of multi-entity tax structuring, grounded in the deep-research build-spec (OECD transfer pricing, BEPS, Pillar Two). NOT a fitted model of any real company, and NOT tax advice.
K2 the money-flows are real intercompany transactions: royalty → IPCo, rent → PropCo, management fee → ServiceCo, interest → FinCo, dividend → parent. On consolidation they net toward zero — but each moves profit between tax jurisdictions.
K3 Pillar Two (2024+): a 15% global minimum effective tax. Route profit to a sub-15% haven and a TOP-UP tax claws the difference back — the red flow. The old "haven" play hits a hard floor. This is current law, not a trick.
K4 transfer-pricing wall: royalty+interest can't exceed the profit that exists. Push past ~90% of EBITDA and the arrangement is non-arm's-length — a tax authority demolishes it. The structure SNAPS.
K5 ATAD interest cap: interest is deductible only up to ~30% of EBITDA. Over-lever the group and the excess debt buys no shield.
K6 unit economics (AUV, EBITDA margin, royalty/rent/fee %) are industry order-of-magnitude from the research; tune to real 10-K / franchise-disclosure data before relying on any number.
K7 the exact curves are illustrative; the DIRECTIONS and the three walls are the honest lesson. Stamp is a signature, not a claim.
