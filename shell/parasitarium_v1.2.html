honesty (K1–K9) — real theory, honest limits:
K1 this is a coupled-ODE toy (host H, parasite P, flora M, immunity I) + a between-host R₀ readout. it reproduces the shape of the standard theory, not any specific pathogen's numbers.
K2 the engine is the transmission–virulence trade-off (Anderson & May 1982; Ewald 1983/94; Frank 1996; Alizon et al. 2009). R₀ = β(v)/(α(v)+γ+μ). β saturates in replication; α (host death) rises with it → an interior optimum v* > 0. Evolution does NOT drive virulence to zero. "A good parasite spares its host" is folklore, not law.
K3 the transmission mode moves v*: needs-a-mobile-host (respiratory) → low v*; waterborne/durable & vector-borne → high v* (an immobilised host still spreads / is bitten more); vertical (parent→child) → v*≈0 → mutualism; manipulate/parasitoid → host death IS the transmission event → v* maxes.
K4 restraint is mostly selected, not chosen. Kinship (rel): one clonal lineage = prudent (kin selection); many strains = tragedy of the commons → over-exploitation. Quorum/latency = hold virulence off until it pays (QS, lysogeny). Nutritional immunity (iron) & colonization resistance = restraint imposed from outside the parasite.
K5 much "virulence" is immunopathology — your own response, not the bug (sepsis, cytokine storm, cerebral malaria). Tolerance (Medzhitov, Soares, Schneider) caps damage-per-parasite without clearing it → peaceful coexistence. Resistance clears harder but fuels the arms race & self-damage.
K6 the microbial "kg": the honest figure is ≈0.2 kg of bacteria (~3.8×10¹³ cells, ~1.3:1 vs human cells — Sender, Fuchs & Milo 2016), plus ~10¹³⁺ virus particles, mostly bacteriophages policing the bacteria (kill-the-winner). The famous "1–2 kg" is an overestimate. target ≠ result.
K7 outcomes are illustrative regimes, not predictions. bistability (controlled vs runaway load) and the epidemic threshold R₀=1 are real bifurcations; the exact tipping points here are tuned for legibility.
K8 motion is opt-in (Curse 13): it starts still; you press begin. the price is always paid — over-extraction self-terminates (host dies → parasite dies with it) unless the mode lets it cash out at death.
K9 the 🌍 epidemic scene is an agent SIR-D on ~520 hosts: each infection carries a virulence that mutates on transmission, so mean virulence evolves up the same R₀ curve to v* (the pink marker climbs to the gold ◆). recovery/death rates match the curve, so where it lands ≈ what the curve predicts. two honest caveats: (i) very lethal modes plateau below the deterministic v* — near-lethal strains keep going locally extinct in a finite pool (demographic stochasticity), a real effect, not a bug; (ii) R₀=1 is a live transcritical bifurcation — drop transmissibility and the chain truly dies out.
