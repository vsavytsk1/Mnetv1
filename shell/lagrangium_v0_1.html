Why it looks like that

The compact form — all of it

ℒ = −¼ FμνFμν
   + i ψ̄ D̸̸ ψ
   + ψ̄i yij ψj φ + h.c.
   + |Dμφ|2 − V(φ)
Every single term in the wall of algebra is inside one of those four lines. Nothing else is added. The expansion adds no physics.

The three reasons it explodes

1. Written after symmetry breaking. In the compact form, W/Z masses and every Higgs coupling live inside |Dμφ|². Break the symmetry and expand, and each becomes its own explicit term — that is most of sector 2.
2. Written in Rξ gauge. Fixing the gauge this way conjures Faddeev–Popov ghosts (sector 5: X+, X−, X0, Y, and Ḡ in sector 1) and leaves the Goldstones φ+, φ−, φ0 visible throughout sector 2.
3. Every index written out. μ, ν, a, b, c, λ, κ, i, j — expanded by hand instead of left implicit in Fμν and D̸̸.

The price (sector 5, and the φ terms)

The ghosts are not particles. Nobody will ever detect an X+. They exist to cancel the unphysical polarizations that gauge fixing lets into the loops. The Goldstones are likewise eaten by W± and Z.

In unitary gauge all of it vanishes and the Lagrangian shrinks dramatically — but then loop calculations become brutal. So you pay, in terms on the page, for a form you can actually compute with.

The price is always paid. Here it is paid in ink.

What is actually free

The SM has 19 free parameters (no neutrino masses): 9 fermion masses · 3 CKM angles + 1 phase · 3 gauge couplings · θQCD · Higgs mass + vev.
Add Dirac neutrino masses → 26. Majorana → 28.

Every other symbol on that wall is forced by gauge symmetry once those are fixed. The bulk is not freedom — it is consequence.

Honest boundary
