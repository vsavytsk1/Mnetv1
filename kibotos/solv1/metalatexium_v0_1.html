THE BOX THAT PAYS

A source-agnostic metamaterial power-stack laboratory. The goal is a cooler-scale solid-state box that conditions a persistent free-energy source into 220 V RMS at 60 Hz without a steam turbine. Every visible relation uses a target, map, approximation, limit, or residual — never a naked equality claim.

rejectedSymmetry is the valve, not the fuel. Broken inversion symmetry can permit rectification and shift current. At global equilibrium, detailed balance removes sustained directed work. The box must expose a real source port and a real heat-rejection surface.
