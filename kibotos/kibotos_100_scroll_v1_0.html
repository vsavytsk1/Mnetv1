II · the wall — who is even allowed in the room

EXACT Pure bookkeeping. The century’s energy bill:

$$ E \;=\; P_e\,T \;=\; 100\ \text{W}\times 3.156\times10^{9}\ \text{s} \;=\; 3.156\times10^{11}\ \text{J} \;\approx\; 87{,}660\ \text{kWh}. $$

Chemistry’s best honest density sits near \( e_{\text{chem}} \approx 5\times10^{7}\ \text{J/kg} \). Then

\( m_{\text{chem}} \gtrsim \dfrac{E}{e_{\text{chem}}} \approx 6{,}312\ \text{kg} \quad (100\ \text{W}_e), \qquad 63.1\ \text{t} \quad (1\ \text{kW}_e). \)

A 200 kg cooler full of the finest fuel delivers \( \approx 3.2\ \text{W} \) for a century. Chemistry is excluded by three orders of magnitude. Nuclear binding energy — about \(10^{6}\times\) chemical — is the only admitted source. The kettle was never the crime; the ledger was.

III · the law of the broken symmetry

Every generator humanity has ever built obeys one sentence:

\( \boxed{\ P_{\text{out}} \;\lesssim\; \eta(\text{broken symmetry}) \times \Phi(\text{price paid})\ } \)
a rectifying asymmetry, multiplied by a free-energy gradient somebody paid to hold open. Dam: gravity gradient × blade chirality. PV: photon flux × junction asymmetry. RTG: decay heat × Seebeck asymmetry. Remove either factor and the output is zero.

EXACT Neumann’s principle. Every material property tensor \( \mathsf T \) must be invariant under the crystal’s point group \(G\):

$$ \mathsf T \;=\; g\,\mathsf T\,g^{\!\top}\quad \forall\, g\in G. $$

Consequence: in a centrosymmetric medium every odd-rank response tensor vanishes — no piezoelectricity, no diode, no bulk photovoltaic current. The field only flows where the symmetry is deliberately wounded. That is your sentence, promoted to a theorem. Curie said it first: the dissymmetry of the effect must pre-exist in its causes.

EXACT The second ingredient is mandatory. Feynman’s ratchet: an asymmetry alone, sitting at equilibrium, extracts nothing — the second law strikes the term. Broken symmetry rectifies; only a paid gradient supplies. The Prime Axiom in thermodynamic dress: the price is always paid. Always.

ESTABLISHED The blue-LED witness. Wurtzite III-nitrides carry a spontaneous polarization because the lattice breaks inversion along its axis. Butt AlGaN against GaN and the polarization discontinuity \( \sigma \triangleq \Delta P\!\cdot\!\hat n \) conjures a two-dimensional electron sea of density \( n_s \approx \sigma/e \sim 10^{13}\ \text{cm}^{-2} \) — with no doping at all. The electrons rush in to screen the wound. The impossible-blue-LED lattice family is the cleanest existing proof that symmetry breaking alone summons carriers; the ASML fab is simply the forge where humanity pays joules and PhD-tears to wound crystals with 2 nm precision.

The Fibonacci stack’s optics is ruled by an integer-born recursion, exactly the temperament of \( \mathcal M_{\text{light}} \). With \( x_n \triangleq \tfrac12\operatorname{tr} M_n \) for the concatenated transfer matrices:

$$ x_{n+1} \;=\; 2\,x_n x_{n-1} \;-\; x_{n-2}, \qquad I \;=\; x_{n+1}^2 + x_n^2 + x_{n-1}^2 \;-\; 2\,x_{n+1}x_n x_{n-1} \;-\; 1 \;\;\text{is conserved,} $$

$$ I(\nu) \;=\; \tfrac14\!\left(\kappa - \kappa^{-1}\right)^{2}\sin^{4}\!\delta, \qquad \kappa \;=\; \frac{n_A}{n_B},\quad \delta \;=\; \frac{\pi}{2}\,\frac{\nu}{\nu_0}. $$
