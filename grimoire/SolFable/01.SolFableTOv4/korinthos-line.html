The countdown is exact to the millisecond. The bus is not. A rural KTEL run carries a real-world jitter of roughly ±10 minutes — early is as likely as late, and an early bus is the one that strands you. Treat the number above as the latest you may arrive, not the moment to leave. Honest, not hidden.
Nothing runs on Sunday, either direction, either end. A taxi between the two runs about €10–15; from the railway station to the ruins, about €7–10.
Last bus back from the village is 17:30. A bus leaves Κόρινθος at 21:00 but does not return that evening — it sleeps in Αρχαία Κόρινθος. Take the 21:00 out only if you are sleeping out there too.
Saturdays thin out to three each way. Sundays are zero. And on 11 September the winter timetable takes over from the summer one — the day you leave. Call 27410 75410 (ext. 1) before you count on the last leg.
One that isn't on the bus map: Acrocorinth — the fortress, the Upper Peirene spring — has no bus at all, and the gate shuts at 15:30. It's a taxi or a 45-minute climb from the village. Plan the moon around the gate, not the other way round.
