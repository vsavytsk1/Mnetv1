The conjecture. A single binary operator, eml(x,y) = exp(x) − ln(y), together with the constant 1, generates the full 36-primitive repertoire of a scientific calculator — arithmetic, powers, logs, trig, hyperbolics, their inverses, and the constants e, π, i. Grammar: S → 1 | eml(S,S). A two-button calculator suffices. Internally, computation must pass through ℂ.

What this shell does. Every node in the spiral below is defined only from earlier nodes, terminating in eml and 1 — so each one genuinely lowers to a pure EML binary tree. On load, this page evaluates every chain over ℂ at 16 deterministic sample points and measures agreement against native references in correct digits (D = −log₁₀ rel err, capped 15.9 — the float64 ceiling). A node earns its glow; nothing is drawn as true that was not measured here. Target and result are never the same line.

IThe conjecture — one operator, one constant▶
external Continuous mathematics never had a NAND. Boolean logic collapses to one gate; the calculator's 36 buttons — constants π, e, i, −1, 1, 2, x, y; functions exp, ln, 1/x, x/2, −x, √, x², σ, the six trig, the six hyperbolic and their inverses; operations +, −, ×, /, log, pow, avg, hypot — were assumed irreducibly many. The paper's claim: they all collapse to
eml(x, y) = exp(x) − ln(y)   with the constant 1   ·   grammar S → 1 | eml(S, S)
exact The seeds are one-liners: e = eml(1,1) since ln 1 = 0, and eˣ = eml(x,1). The first non-trivial descent is the logarithm — Eq. (5) of the paper:
ln z = eml(1, eml(eml(1, z), 1))   ≡   e − ln(eᵉ / z)   ·   RPN: 1 1 z E 1 E E  (K = 7)
external From there the discovery run bootstraps: each new primitive is composed from already-derived ones, spiraling outward from the LUCA — subtraction, negation, the integers, arithmetic, powers, then σ and hypot, the hyperbolic family, and finally — since i = √−1 must be manufactured from ln(−1) — the circular family. Computation passes through ℂ by necessity; the paper argues a purely real continuous Sheffer seems impossible. Completeness rests on the paper's Supplementary Part II (symbolic checks in Mathematica, numeric cross-checks in C / NumPy / PyTorch / mpmath, and a constructive proof sketch) — cited here, not re-proved here.
design This shell re-derives all 33 primitives through its own reuse chain (each node from earlier nodes; the true discovery chain is the paper's SI Table S2, not reproduced here) and re-verifies every one, live, below your cursor. The ordering of the spiral follows Fig. 1 by eye. hypothesis The paper speculates EML is the tip of an iceberg — a ternary variant may need no distinguished constant at all. Cousins already known: edl(x,y) = exp(x)/ln(y) with constant e, and −eml(y,x) with constant −∞.
