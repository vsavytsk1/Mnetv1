Before you go

  • Nothing runs on SundayNeither direction. A taxi between the two is roughly €10–15.
  • Last bus back is 17:30A bus leaves Korinthos at 21:00 but does not return the same evening. Don't get stuck at the ruins.
  • Saturdays are thinnerOut at 08:30, 11:00, 14:00 only. Back at 09:00, 11:30, 14:30 only.
  • Check which bayThe local bus doesn't always leave from the same spot as the Athens coaches. Ask at the ticket window when you buy.
