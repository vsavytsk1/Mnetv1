# I. FROM COLOR CHARGE TO HADRONS

## The local $SU(3)$ gauge principle

### Fields and transformations

Let the quark multiplet $q_f(x)$ carry flavor $f$ and transform in the fundamental representation of $SU(3)_c$,

$$
q_f(x)\mapsto q'_f(x)=U(x)q_f(x),\qquad U(x)=e^{i\theta^a(x)T^a},
$$

with Hermitian generators normalized by

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(T^aT^b)=\frac12\delta^{ab},\qquad \left[T^a,T^b\right]=if^{abc}T^c.
$$

The ordinary derivative of $q$ is not covariant because $\partial_\mu U\neq0$. Introduce the gauge field

$$
A_\mu=A_\mu^aT^a,\qquad D_\mu=\partial_\mu-ig_sA_\mu,
$$

and require $D'_\mu q'=U D_\mu q$. Expanding the left-hand side gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
D'_\mu q'&=(\partial_\mu-ig_sA'_\mu)Uq\\
&=(\partial_\mu U)q+U\partial_\mu q-ig_sA'_\mu Uq.
\end{aligned}
$$

Equating this with $U(\partial_\mu-ig_sA_\mu)q$ for arbitrary $q$ yields

$$
\boxed{A'_\mu=UA_\mu U^{-1}-\frac{i}{g_s}(\partial_\mu U)U^{-1}
=UA_\mu U^{-1}+\frac{i}{g_s}U(\partial_\mu U^{-1}).}
$$

The inhomogeneous derivative term is the price of local symmetry.

### Curvature from a commutator

Define the field strength by

$$
\left[D_\mu,D_\nu\right]=-ig_sF_{\mu\nu}.
$$

Direct expansion gives

$$
F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu A_\nu-\partial_\nu A_\mu-ig_s\left[A_\mu,A_\nu\right],
$$

therefore

$$
\boxed{F^a_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu A^a_\nu-\partial_\nu A^a_\mu+g_sf^{abc}A^b_\mu A^c_\nu.}
$$

Because $D'_\mu=UD_\mu U^{-1}$,

$$
F'_{\mu\nu}=UF_{\mu\nu}U^{-1};
$$

hence $\operatorname{Tr}(F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu})$ is gauge invariant.

### The QCD Lagrangian

With quark mass matrix $M_q$,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{QCD}}=-\frac14F^a_{\mu\nu}F^{a\mu\nu}+\sum_f\bar q_f(i\gamma^\mu D_\mu-m_f)q_f+\mathcal{L}_\theta.}
$$

The topological term is

$$
\mathcal{L}_\theta=\theta\,\frac{g_s^2}{32\pi^2}F^a_{\mu\nu}\widetilde F^{a\mu\nu},\qquad
\widetilde F^{\mu\nu}=\frac12\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}F_{\rho\sigma}.
$$

Variation with respect to $\bar q$ and $A_\nu^a$ yields

$$
\begin{aligned}
(i\gamma^\mu D_\mu-m_f)q_f&=0,\\
(D_\mu F^{\mu\nu})^a&=g_s\sum_f\bar q_f\gamma^\nu T^a q_f.
\end{aligned}
$$

The geometric Bianchi identity follows from the Jacobi identity,

$$
D_{\lambda}F_{\mu\nu}+D_{\mu}F_{\nu\lambda}+D_{\nu}F_{\lambda\mu}=0.
$$

def tex_escape(value: Any) -> str:
    text = str(value)
    for old, new in [
        ("\\", r"\textbackslash{}"), ("&", r"\&"), ("%", r"\%"),
        ("$", r"\$"), ("#", r"\#"), ("_", r"\_"),
        ("{", r"\{"), ("}", r"\}"), ("^", r"\^{}"), ("~", r"\~{}"),
    ]:
        text = text.replace(old, new)
    return text


def summarize(result: Any) -> str:
    if isinstance(result, dict):
        items = []
        for key in sorted(result):
            value = result[key]
            if isinstance(value, (dict, list)):
                value_text = json.dumps(value, sort_keys=True, separators=(",", ":"))
            else:
                value_text = str(value)
            items.append(f"{key}={value_text}")
        return "; ".join(items)
    return str(result)


def write_table(root: Path) -> None:
    receipt = json.loads((root / RECEIPT).read_text(encoding="utf-8"))

def sha256(path: Path) -> str:
    digest = hashlib.sha256()
    with path.open("rb") as handle:
        for chunk in iter(lambda: handle.read(1 << 20), b""):
            digest.update(chunk)
    return digest.hexdigest()
